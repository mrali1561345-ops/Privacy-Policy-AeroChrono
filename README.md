# Privacy Policy - AeroChrono

**Last updated:** October 07, 2026

**AeroChrono** ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application collects, uses, and safeguards your information.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Use
AeroChrono is designed to respect user privacy. We do not require account registration, nor do we collect, store, or transmit any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.

## 2. Local Data Storage
All timer settings, session history logs, and app preferences created within AeroChrono are stored locally on your device. This data is not uploaded to external servers or shared with third parties.

## 3. Third-Party Services
The app may utilize standard platform features provided by Android/iOS operating systems. We do not integrate third-party analytics or advertising frameworks that track your activity across other apps or websites.

## 4. Permissions
AeroChrono may request minimal system permissions strictly necessary for functional performance, such as:
* **Vibration / Haptic Feedback:** To provide physical feedback during button presses or timer completion.
* **Keep Awake:** To prevent screen timeout while active timers are running.

## 5. Children's Privacy
AeroChrono does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. The application is safe for general audiences.

## 6. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
